Zhen Cui

PhD student in Physics, Westlake University

My research connects exciton physics with photonic devices: I design and fabricate planar DBR microcavities to reach the strong-coupling regime, where light and matter hybridize into exciton–polaritons, and engineer these quasiparticles toward optoelectronic and sensing applications.

Research

Microcavity strong coupling

Exciton–polariton physics in microcavities with perovskite and other gain media: dispersion characterization, Rabi splitting, and the optical properties of hybrid light–matter states.

DBR design & fabrication

Full-stack cavity engineering: inverse design of dielectric stacks from reflectance spectra, and complete fabrication workflows on two deposition platforms (e-beam evaporation and ion-beam sputtering).

Polariton devices & sensing

Toward applications of hybrid excitations: polariton-based optoelectronic devices, and microcavity / metacavity photonic sensors for label-free biosensing.
